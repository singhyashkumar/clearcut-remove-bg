A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

The small print, made clear.

Is it free to use?

This version is free, with no watermark or account requirement. Google sign-in is optional and does not change processing or unlock quotas today. Processing uses your device, so there is no per-photo AI API charge. Hosting and bandwidth still have costs and limits.

Are my images uploaded or saved?

No image upload service, analytics, or image storage is used. Your photos, masks, and edits remain in browser memory. Clearing the photo or closing this tab discards them. AI models may be cached by your browser for future visits.

Why does the first photo take longer?

The first run downloads a model of approximately 98 MB, or up to 473 MB in detailed mode. Browser caching can help subsequent runs. Older devices may be slow or run out of memory. Use a current Chrome or Edge browser for the initial release.

Will every edge be perfect?

No. Hair, glass, shadows, low contrast, and multiple subjects can need manual refinement. Both models work at 512px internally; the output can keep the photo’s dimensions, but that does not add native-resolution AI detail. This release targets desktop Chrome and Edge; iOS is not supported yet.

Can I use the downloads commercially?

The engine and configured models use Apache-2.0 and MIT licenses. You are responsible for the rights to the photos you choose. See the included third-party notices for component attribution.
